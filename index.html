What is Vclarity?

Vclarity is a Chrome extension for inspecting web interfaces, understanding visual regions, and capturing interactions. Version 0.2.0 adds video capture and AI-assisted HTML, CSS, and JavaScript generation. It is currently available as a developer preview.

How does Live Inspect work?

Press Alt + I, hover over an element, then select it. Vclarity reads its real DOM and computed CSS, including typography, colors, dimensions, padding, margins, and border radius. These measurements are labeled Exact (DOM).

How is Ghost Inspect different?

Live Inspect measures a specific DOM element. With Ghost Inspect (Alt + S), you draw a rectangle around a larger visual region. Image-based AI analysis helps explain the composition and reconstruct its structure. AI estimates are not exact DOM measurements.

What can Video Capture record?

Capture visible website animations, transitions, hover states, and interactions. Hold region records your drawn rectangle while you keep the mouse button held and stationary; release to stop. Record tab captures the visible tab with a recording toggle and Stop control. Both stop automatically at 30 seconds.

Does it record audio?

No. Neither Hold region nor Record tab records audio.

Why is there a 30-second limit?

Version 0.2.0 is designed for short interface interactions. Recording is capped at 30 seconds per capture in both modes, and stops automatically at that limit. Capture a focused interaction for each analysis.

What happens when I send a video to AI?

After you grant permission to send captures to AI, a completed video is submitted through the configured Vclarity API for analysis. The extension displays its processing status, motion observations, and generated code. The service must receive the capture to analyze it; only send content you intend to share. This page’s demos do not upload anything.

Can I copy and download the generated code?

Yes. You can copy or download the generated HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. AI output is an interpretation of the visible interface and behavior, so review and test it before use. Pixel-perfect source reconstruction and code accuracy are not guaranteed.
